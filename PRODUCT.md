# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Internal partners at Tapestry Inc. (product managers, engineers, cross-functional leads, new hires, and design team members). Context: desktop, during working hours, often arriving via a Slack link or onboarding doc. They need a quick orientation — who is on this team, what do they own, how do they work, where do I go to find things.

## Product Purpose

tapestry.design is the design team's internal home. It makes the team visible, communicates what they own and how they work, and gives partners and new hires a single reliable place to orient themselves. Success: a visitor can describe the team's scope, find the right person, and locate the Figma source-of-truth — without needing to ask on Slack.

## Brand Personality

Precise. Connected. Grounded.

The team behind the interfaces — not a creative studio portfolio, not a tech startup, not a design system documentation site. A working team that takes craft seriously. The site should feel like the team itself: capable, direct, and confident without being loud.

## Anti-references

- Generic design agency portfolio (big hero typography, black-and-white with one accent, Fraunces serif, ruled columns — the editorial-typographic lane)
- Warm cream / sand / parchment backgrounds — the 2026 AI default
- SaaS dashboard aesthetics (metric callouts, sidebar navigation, dense card grids)
- Luxury brand affectations (gold, marble, monogram marks, ornate type)
- Numbered section scaffolding on every section (01 · About / 02 · Process) used as reflexive AI grammar

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach** — a design team's own site is the most direct demonstration of craft. Every layout decision, type choice, and spacing call is evidence.
2. **Clarity over completeness** — answer the most important questions fast. Resist filling every section with everything.
3. **Earned structure** — sections, numbers, and labels exist when they carry information, not as decoration or scaffolding.
4. **Confident restraint** — not minimalism for minimalism's sake, but every element should be there because removing it would make the page worse.
5. **The shader is the voice** — the Sunrise shader (warm orange orb, liquify-interactive) is the one expressive moment. Everything else serves it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast on background. Interactive elements keyboard-navigable. Reduced-motion: shader animation should respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Section headings use semantic HTML (h1, h2). No color as the sole status signal.
